# Lost Ark Party Product Notes

## Product

Lost Ark Party is a Korean operations tool for fixed Lost Ark raid groups.
It helps raid leaders and raid members coordinate the weekly reset cycle without
mixing public guide content with private group data.

## Primary Users

- 공대장: confirms weekly schedules, reviews availability, manages raid
  composition, checks failed notifications, and maintains group settings.
- 공대원: enters weekly availability, applies to open raid signups, updates
  character homework, and checks group/member context.

## Core Experience

The first authenticated screen should answer one question: "What do I need to
do before the next raid starts?" The dashboard separates work into 공대장 업무
and 공대원 업무 while still keeping both roles visible because leaders also
participate as members.

## Design Direction

- Operational, dense, and scannable rather than promotional.
- Role-based sections before feature-by-feature navigation.
- Compact status badges for counts that need action.
- Neutral slate surfaces with teal primary actions, amber warnings, emerald
  success, and rose danger states.
- Cards stay at 8px radius or less. Avoid nested card stacks and decorative
  gradients.
